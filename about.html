ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Helping the CTU Community
Reconnect with What Matters

A secure, official digital portal built to streamline the process of reporting, tracking, and recovering lost items within Cebu Technological University.

What We Do

A Smarter Way to Recover Lost Items

The CTU Lost & Found System is an official digital platform designed for students, faculty, and staff of Cebu Technological University. It replaces the traditional manual lost-and-found process with a fast, organized, and transparent system.

Whether you've misplaced your ID, wallet, gadget, or any personal belonging, this portal connects finders and owners within the campus community quickly and securely.

Our Mission

To provide the CTU community with a reliable, accessible, and transparent lost-and-found management system that promotes honesty, accountability, and community trust.

Our Vision

To be the leading campus lost-and-found solution that fosters a culture of integrity and mutual care among every member of the CTU community.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, and community service drive every feature of this system. We believe every lost item deserves a chance to find its way home.

How It Works

Simple Steps to Recover Your Belongings

01

Create an Account

Sign up using a valid email address to access the full portal features.

02

Report an Item

Submit a lost or found report with photos, description, and location details.

03

Wait for Confirmation

Wait for the admin to validate and confirm the claim requests.

04

Claim Your Item

Coordinate with the admin office to verify identity and retrieve your belonging.
